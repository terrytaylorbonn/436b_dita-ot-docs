Chapter 3: Storage and Management

Processed data is stored in a centralized repository, which may include data lakes, warehouses, or cloud-based storage solutions.

The storage architecture is optimized for performance, scalability, and cost-effectiveness. Metadata management tools catalog datasets, track lineage, and facilitate search and discovery. Access policies and role-based permissions protect sensitive information and ensure compliance with organizational standards. Backup and disaster recovery strategies are implemented to safeguard against data loss. See Chapter 4: Monitoring and Optimization for more on system health and recovery. Ongoing monitoring and maintenance routines help maintain system health and adapt to evolving storage needs.
